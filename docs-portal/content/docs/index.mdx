### Core Operational Principles

1. **Strict Temporal Purity (Zero Future Leakage)**
   * *Challenge*: Standard random cross-validation leaks future autocorrelation into historical training slices, yielding unrealistic 99% accuracy in the lab that collapses in production.
   * *NetPredict Solution*: Strict walk-forward chronological train/test splitting with an embargo gap, combined with backward-only rolling window aggregations.

2. **Decoupled Anomaly Detection vs. Predictive Forecasting**
   * *Challenge*: Real-world networks confuse "what is broken right now" with "what is developing towards failure".
   * *NetPredict Solution*: Unsupervised Isolation Forest isolates immediate state degradation, while calibrated LightGBM predictors anticipate forward-looking risk.

3. **Distribution-Free Conformal Uncertainty Guarantees**
   * *Challenge*: Point predictions fail to provide operators with actionable risk bounds during volatile microbursts.
   * *NetPredict Solution*: Split Conformal Prediction provides 90% finite-sample coverage guarantees on forecasted RTT and packet drop cliffs.

4. **Transparent Root-Cause Explainability**
   * *Challenge*: Black-box neural models are unacceptable for critical core routers.
   * *NetPredict Solution*: Exact polynomial-time TreeSHAP decomposes every prediction into human-readable operator root causes.
