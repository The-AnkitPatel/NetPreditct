### The 4-Quadrant Operational State Matrix

By decoupling both signals, NetPredict classifies every telemetry instant into one of four deterministic operational states:

1. **Quadrant 1: Nominal Operations**
   * *Anomaly*: `FALSE` | *Prediction*: `LOW RISK`
   * *Status*: Healthy network baseline. No intervention required.

2. **Quadrant 2: Early Warning (Impending Degradation)**
   * *Anomaly*: `FALSE` | *Prediction*: `HIGH RISK`
   * *Status*: **The holy grail of network operations.** Metrics are still within standard threshold limits, but the temporal delay gradient ($\Delta RTT/\Delta t$) and queue stress indicate that buffers will overflow within 15 minutes. Proactive rerouting prevents downtime.

3. **Quadrant 3: Transient Anomaly (False Alarm Filter)**
   * *Anomaly*: `TRUE` | *Prediction*: `LOW RISK`
   * *Status*: A single ping burst or scheduled maintenance poll triggered an outlier score, but the temporal trajectory is self-recovering. NetPredict suppresses alarm paging to eliminate operator fatigue.

4. **Quadrant 4: Critical Failure Cascade**
   * *Anomaly*: `TRUE` | *Prediction*: `HIGH RISK`
   * *Status*: Active link degradation combined with guaranteed future packet loss. Immediate automated failover triggered.
